Waypoint 06 — Before you ask

Frequently asked questions

Room assignments will be shuffled to encourage everyone to meet and fellowship with different sisters and brothers. Each room will have 7 or 10 people.
Yes. Meals will include a mix of meat and vegetable options. If you have specific dietary restrictions or allergies, please inform the region coordinators ahead of time.
Each room will have a designated leader/point person. A group chat will be created once everyone is checked in for easier communication, and retreat coordinators will also be available as points of contact throughout the retreat.
Please do not rely on Wi-Fi during the retreat. We encourage everyone to prepare mobile data as needed — and most importantly, enjoy the fellowship and unplug when you can!
Brothers are not allowed inside the sisters' rooms, and vice versa. Friday lights out is 10:00 PM; Saturday lights out is 12:00 MN. Please do not roam around the camp after lights out — let's respect the camp, our roommates, and everyone's need for rest.
Seats will be pre-assigned. Please check your assigned seat before boarding.
The ₱5,200 registration fee does not include entrance fees. We encourage everyone to bring sufficient pocket money for entrance fees, food, transportation, and other personal expenses.
